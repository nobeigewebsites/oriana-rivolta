ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Stories beyond the familiar.

Oriana Rivolta is the pseudonym of an independent historical fiction author who has chosen to remain anonymous, allowing the stories rather than the person behind them to take centre stage.

Fascinated by forgotten histories, disputed truths and the voices lost between the pages of time, Oriana explores the question at the heart of every great mystery: What if the history we know is only part of the story?

Through richly imagined characters, historical settings and unexpected perspectives, her novels invite readers to look beyond familiar accounts, question long-held assumptions and rediscover the people whose stories history may have overlooked.

Her debut novel, The First Thread, opens The Forgotten Truths, a four-book historical mystery series exploring the secrets that survive across generations.

But the mystery doesn't end with the final chapter.

Oriana believes fiction has the power not only to entertain, but to awaken curiosity. Each story is an invitation to explore the genuine history behind the fiction, investigate unanswered questions and perhaps discover an entirely different perspective.

The identity behind the name Oriana Rivolta remains private by choice.
