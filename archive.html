THE HISTORY BEHIND THE FICTION

One story. Many perspectives.

Every Oriana Rivolta novel begins with history and follows imagination into the spaces between the records.

The Archive is where readers are invited to look deeper: to explore genuine historical events, examine disputed accounts and see where the novelist's imagination takes a different path.

Not to replace documented history with fiction, but to inspire curiosity about the people and perspectives behind it.
